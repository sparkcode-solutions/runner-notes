# Runner Notes

**A running app that doesn't clap for you.**

---

Strava counts your splits.
Watches measure your zones.
Your friends' feeds are full of trophies.

You don't need another app telling you that you ran fast.
You already know.

---

## Runner Notes is different.

It's the running app for people who run **to think**, not to win.

A private journal where every run is a moment, not a metric.
A coach that asks the right question instead of throwing kudos at you.
A place to remember what the wind felt like, what you were chewing over,
why you went out at 5am even though it was cold.

---

## Built around one simple idea

> **A run is a thought, not a number.**

So we made an app that treats it that way.

- **Run Moments** — each run is a card you can flip open. Stats on the front. Story on the back.
- **The Reflection** — an on-device AI that reads your journal and your run, then asks one curious question. Never says "great job." Never asks for more pace. Just listens, and reflects.
- **Run Snaps** — capture the puddle, the sunrise, the dog you saw. Photos belong to the moment, not a feed.
- **The Coach** — a quiet presence on your home screen. Knows the weather. Knows the time. Knows when you should rest and when you're ready to push. Greets you with one sentence, picked for you, today.
- **Trails** — name the loops that mean something to you. "The Long Way Home." "Sunday Forest." "When I Couldn't Sleep."
- **Vibes, not leaderboards** — a run can be heavy, in flow, or a struggle. We don't grade them.

## What you won't find here

No followers.
No likes.
No leaderboard.
No notifications begging you to come back.
No 30-day streak guilt.
No friends you'd rather not race against.
No data sold to anyone, ever.

Your runs stay on your phone. Full stop.

---

## Who it's for

- The runner who deletes Strava every six months and reinstalls it anyway.
- The journaler who never quite knows what to write at the top of a blank page.
- The thinker who solves their best problems somewhere between kilometre two and four.
- The person who still remembers their best run not by its pace, but by what they were thinking on the way back.

---

## How it feels

Open the app. The orb breathes.

> *"Crisp air outside. Perfect for clearing the mind."*

Swipe right — you're running.
Swipe left — the coach is listening.

When you stop, write a line. Or don't.
Either way, the moment is yours, saved, ready to flip back to next month
when you've forgotten what April felt like.

---

## A coach worth keeping

Most apps shout.
Ours notices.

> *"You mentioned the wind was biting today, but your pace stayed steady. Where did that focus come from?"*

That's the whole pitch. A mirror, not a megaphone.
